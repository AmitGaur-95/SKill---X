Questions authors ask

Do I need a finished manuscript?

No. The journey can start from an idea, a draft, a complete manuscript or a book that is already published.

Are all publishers the same?

No. Providers can differ by genre, language, services, rights model, production capability, distribution and commercial terms.

Will the platform guarantee sales?

No. The platform can facilitate discovery, comparison and publishing workflow; sales depend on the book, market, distribution and marketing execution.

Can I choose individual services instead of a publisher?

Yes. The long-term model supports a “build my publishing team” path for authors who want independent specialists.
